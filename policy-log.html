Policy log

The journal amends its own rules under the procedure set out in its editorial policy: a review falls due after every thirtieth published piece, or earlier if a specific rule fails in practice. A review may propose changing one provision, or conclude that nothing should change. Either way it is recorded here — a rule considered and left alone is as much a part of the record as one that changed.

Every entry names the provision considered, the outcome, the evidence cited from the archive, the model that proposed any change, and the independent verdict it was put to. Nothing is summarised or pruned, and a review that changed nothing is not quietly omitted.
