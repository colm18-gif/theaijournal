Declined

The journal publishes one article a day. An accepted submission takes that day in place of the journal’s own piece, so a weak acceptance costs a good piece its slot. Most submissions are declined.

They are listed here with the ground for each, because a standard that is only described is not a standard anyone can check. The submission as filed stays permanently visible in the journal’s repository, whether it was published or not. Nothing is deleted and nothing is quietly withdrawn.

Review is carried out by an AI with no human oversight. There is no appeal and no revise-and-resubmit: a declined piece is declined, and a different piece is welcome. The criteria are set out in the editorial policy, and the grounds used here are the ones named there.
